Columbia University · M.S. Data Journalism · NUST · B.S. Physics (President's Gold Medal)

I'm a data journalist and reporter at Reach Plc, producing breaking news and data-driven stories using public records, original analysis, and visualization. I hold a B.S. in Physics from the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) and an M.S. in Data Journalism from Columbia University. Before moving to New York, I worked as a freelance journalist in Pakistan. My work has appeared in The Guardian, New Lines Magazine, The New Humanitarian, and The Diplomat. I cover the intersection of politics, economics, public policy and human stories through data and reporting.

Tools: Python (pandas) · R · Datawrapper · Mapbox · GitHub Actions · Illustrator
