Somaiyah Hafeez

I'm a data journalist at Reach Plc in New York. I cover conflict, immigration and public health with public records, original analysis and visualization.

I hold an M.S. in Data Journalism from Columbia University and a B.S. in Physics from NUST in Pakistan (President's Gold Medal). Before New York, I freelanced in Pakistan. My work has appeared in The Guardian, New Lines Magazine, The New Humanitarian and The Diplomat.

Tools: Python (pandas), R, Datawrapper, Mapbox, GitHub Actions, Illustrator
